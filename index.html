Our Mission

Students teaching students, across every part of India.

Our mission is to build a student-led education movement across India, where privileged students use their time, knowledge, and skills to support children who don't have the same access to quality learning.

So far, our network of young volunteers has helped impact 2,000+ underprivileged children through free academic support, personality development, creative learning activities, workshops, and community programs.

Our goal is to take this model beyond one city and create a scalable system where students can teach students in every part of India — reaching 1 million children by making education more personal, practical, and confidence-building.

Our Story

From one classroom to a national movement.

It started with one moment. Our founder noticed her domestic help deeply worried about her daughter — a bright young girl who, like so many children from underprivileged backgrounds, simply didn't have access to the guidance and encouragement many of us take for granted. That moment raised a hard question: why should a child's dreams be limited by the family or circumstances they're born into?

"We may be a drop in the ocean, but we are what makes the ocean."

What began as the desire to help one child slowly became something larger: a simple idea that students who have access to better education can use their knowledge to help students who don't. That idea became the heart of this foundation.

The children here aren't just learning chapters from textbooks — they're gaining confidence, asking questions, participating, and beginning to believe they can dream bigger. More student volunteers have joined along the way, choosing to spend their own time teaching, mentoring, and creating opportunities for children who need support.

Today, Fly High Foundation provides free academic support, personality development, creative learning, workshops, and community programs — all built on a promise that no child should feel left behind for lacking the right resources or support.

Gauri MuthaFounder, Fly High Foundation
“

I have always believed that education should do more than help a child pass an exam. It should help them ask better questions, build confidence, discover their strengths, and believe that their future can be bigger than their present circumstances.

I grew up in a family where service and community weren't just values we spoke about — they were values I saw in action. My grandfather, Late Mr. Sharad Mansuklal Mutha, has been one of my greatest inspirations. He believed in using whatever we have — time, knowledge, resources, or influence — to support others.

As a student, I was fortunate to receive guidance and opportunities that let me learn freely and dream boldly. But I also saw children around me who were just as bright and capable, yet didn't have access to the same support. That contrast stayed with me, and Fly High Foundation began from that realization.

My goal now is to take Fly High to the next level — to build one of India's largest student-led education movements and reach 1 million children across the country. Fly High is a reflection of the values I grew up with, and the future I want to help build: one where every child, regardless of background, has the chance to learn, grow, and rise.

Gauri MuthaFounder, Fly High Foundation
